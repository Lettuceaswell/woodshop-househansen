The mill

One log. Nothing wasted.

Forty years of building, a bandsaw mill, and a pile of Norway pine from a logger we know. Every board here was pushed through the blade by hand, right on the property in Trego.

The shop barn going up now is 24 by 24, engineered around 8-foot timbers because that's what the mill can cut. Its beams came off this same pile. Deck-rehab jobs bring home reclaimed lumber, and that wood becomes the next thing we build.

“I love what I do. I love creating and building things with wood.”The sawyer
  1. The logSquared into a cant, then sawn into lumber and timbers.
  2. The flitchLive edges left on for shelves, mantels and tabletops.
  3. The slabThe rounded first cut, cut down into camp wood and kindling.
  4. The scrapsOffcuts from furniture builds, saved for the garden and the fire.
  5. The buildingThe shop, the sugar shack and the farm store, framed from the same pile.
